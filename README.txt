Brivis (formerly Rinnai) ducted gas heating and evaporative cooling is controlled from wall-mounted Networker controllers, one for each zone of the house. This app brings them into Homey over your local network: every zone becomes a Homey device with its own measured temperature and setpoint, alongside the controls the whole system shares, being the operating mode, circulation fan and fan speed. Evaporative coolers get the Networker's own two ways of running: Manual, where you set the fan speed and pump, or Auto, where you pick a comfort level and the cooler does the rest, with Homey showing when the pads are pre-wetting and whether the fan and pump are actually running. Changes made at a wall controller are picked up automatically, so Homey always shows what the system is actually doing.

From there your heating and cooling can join your Flows like any other device: warm the bedrooms before the alarm goes off, ease off the setpoint once everyone has left for the day, start the cooler in Auto before a hot afternoon lets the house build up heat, or let a temperature sensor you already own decide.

This app only supports systems using the Rinnai/Brivis Touch™ Wi-Fi Controller, which needs to be installed and reachable on the same network as your Homey. The controller accepts only one connection at a time, so while Homey is controlling it the Rinnai Touch mobile app should not be used: the two would compete for that connection, and either may fail to connect.
