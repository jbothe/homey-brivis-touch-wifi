Brivis ducted gas heating and evaporative cooling is controlled from wall-mounted Networker controllers, one for each zone of the house. This app brings them into Homey over your local network: every zone becomes a Homey device with its own target and measured temperature, alongside the controls the whole system shares, being the operating mode, circulation fan, fan speed and, on evaporative systems, the pump. Changes made at a wall controller, or in the vendor's own phone app, are picked up automatically, so Homey always shows what the system is actually doing.

From there your heating and cooling can join your Flows like any other device: warm the bedrooms before the alarm goes off, ease off the setpoint once everyone has left for the day, switch to fan-only on a mild afternoon, or let a temperature sensor you already own decide. A Brivis Touch Wi-Fi controller module, sold previously as Rinnai Touch, needs to be installed and reachable on the same network as your Homey.
